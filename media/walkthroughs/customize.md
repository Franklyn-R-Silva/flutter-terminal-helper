# Favorite & customize

- Hover a command and click the **★ star** to pin it to the **⭐ Favorites**
  category at the top of the sidebar.
- Your most recently used commands appear under **🕘 Recent** automatically.
- Add your own commands with the `flutterHelper.customCommands` setting — they
  show up in a **⭐ Custom Commands** category.
- Working with FVM? Set `flutterHelper.useFvm` and `flutter`/`dart` commands run
  through `fvm` automatically.
